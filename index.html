About Us

"We Give You More For Your Budget"

Founded in 2021 by Robson Machiva, Diamond Motor Spares was born from a clear vision: to bridge the gap between premium automotive quality and affordability.

We specialize in a comprehensive inventory of spares for all makes and models. Every part we stock is meticulously vetted for durability and performance.

Today, we proudly serve the community through strategic branches in Mutare and Wengezi, providing localized expertise for drivers and mechanics alike.

A Growing Legacy

We are actively expanding our footprint with new branches scheduled to open this year bringing world-class spares closer to your doorstep.

Our Mission

To empower every driver with reliable, high-standard automotive solutions that ensure vehicle longevity without compromising on cost.

Why Choose Us
