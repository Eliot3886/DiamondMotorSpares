About Us

"We Give You More For Your Budget"

Founded in 2021 by Robson Machiva, Diamond Motor Spares was born from a clear vision: to bridge the gap between premium automotive quality and affordability for every vehicle owner.

We specialize in a comprehensive inventory of genuine and aftermarket spares for all makes and models. Whether you are looking for Toyota Hiace (Quantum) parts, Nissan NP200/Hardbody components, or reliable spares for Isuzu, Mazda, and Honda, every part we stock is meticulously vetted for durability.

Today, we proudly serve the Manicaland community through strategic branches in Mutare and Wengezi. We provide localized expertise in engine components, suspension kits, braking systems, and service filters for both light passenger cars and heavy-duty workhorses.

A Growing Legacy

We are actively expanding our footprint with new branches scheduled to open this year—bringing world-class Toyota, Nissan, and Mitsubishi spares closer to your doorstep in Zimbabwe.
