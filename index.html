About Diamond Motor Spares

"We Give You More For Your Budget"

Founded in 2021 by Robson Machiva, Diamond Motor Spares was built on a simple yet powerful vision: to provide high-quality automotive components that keep you on the road without breaking the bank.

We specialize in a comprehensive range of motor spares for all vehicle makes and models. Whether you are performing routine maintenance or a major repair, we source parts that meet the highest standards of reliability and performance.

Currently, we are proud to serve our customers through two established branches in Mutare and Wengezi. Our local presence allows us to understand the specific needs of drivers in these regions and provide fast, expert service.

Our Future

We are not stopping here. At Diamond Motor Spares, we are driven by growth. To better serve the automotive community, we are excited to announce that we will be opening more branches in the coming year, bringing affordable, quality spares to even more locations.

Our Mission

To be the leading provider of affordable, high-quality motor spares in the region, ensuring safety and reliability for every driver.
